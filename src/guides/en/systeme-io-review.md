## What Systeme.io actually is

Most solopreneurs end up stitching together four or five tools: a page builder, an email provider, a course platform, a payment tool and, later, an affiliate tracking tool. Systeme.io's pitch is that you can do all of that in one place. According to the FAQ on its pricing page, a comparable stack (ClickFunnels for funnels, Mailchimp for email, Teachable for courses and Tapfiliate for affiliates) adds up to roughly $245 per month; that comparison is Systeme.io's own marketing math, so treat it as their claim, not ours.

What matters more for a beginner is the structure of the product:

- **Sales funnels**: sequences of pages (opt-in, thank-you, sales, checkout, upsell).
- **Email marketing**: broadcasts (called campaigns), automation rules and workflows.
- **Courses**: host lessons and enroll students.
- **Blog and website**: publish articles and pages on your own domain.
- **Payments**: Stripe, PayPal and other processors are integrated, with **0% transaction fees** from Systeme.io.
- **Your own affiliate program**: let other people promote your products.
- **Link-in-bio page**: a mobile page for your social profiles.

## The free plan in detail

This is the part that matters for anyone following a zero-budget launch. According to the official FAQ, the free plan includes:

- up to **2,000 contacts**;
- **3 sales funnels**;
- **1 blog**, **1 course**, **1 affiliate program** and **1 custom domain**;
- **unlimited emails sent**;
- no expiry, and **no credit card required**.

The constraints are real, and you should know them before building:

- **1 automation rule**;
- **1 email campaign**;
- **500 students** for courses.

What does that mean in practice? One automation rule is enough for the single most important automation in a beginner funnel: "when someone signs up to the funnel, send the delivery email and add a tag". One campaign means you will not build an elaborate multi-branch nurture system on the free plan. For a first lead magnet with a short welcome sequence, it is workable; for a complex, segmented email program, it is not.

## FAQ

**Is Systeme.io really free?**
Yes. The free plan never expires and does not require a credit card, according to the official pricing FAQ. Its limits are 2,000 contacts, 3 funnels, 1 course, 1 automation rule and 1 campaign.

**What happens if I cancel a paid plan?**
Per the official FAQ, you keep your current plan until the next billing date, then the account reverts to the free plan.

**Does Systeme.io take a cut of my sales?**
No. Systeme.io advertises 0% transaction fees on all plans. Your payment processor's own fees still apply.

**How many emails can I send?**
Unlimited on every plan, according to the pricing page.

**When should I upgrade?**
When a limit starts costing you money: more than 2,000 contacts, more than 3 funnels, or the need for more than one automation rule. The Startup plan is $17/month.
