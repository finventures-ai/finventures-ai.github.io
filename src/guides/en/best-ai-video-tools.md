## What "AI video tool" actually covers

The label covers very different jobs. Pick by job, not by hype:

1. **Avatar video** — a digital presenter (stock or your own likeness) reads a script. HeyGen and Synthesia.
2. **Voice** — text-to-speech or voice cloning for narration. ElevenLabs, and built-in voices in HeyGen and Synthesia.
3. **Editing** — cut, caption and clean up real recordings. Descript is the best-known text-based editor.
4. **Generative clips** — short b-roll or scenes generated from prompts. HeyGen's Pro plan lists access to third-party generation models; Synthesia lists b-roll generation that consumes credits.

## A $0 AI video workflow for solopreneurs

You can produce your first month of short videos without paying anything:

1. **Script** with a free AI chat assistant: hook (3 seconds), three value points, a "comment WORD" call-to-action, and a disclosure line if a link is involved.
2. **Test avatar video** on HeyGen's free plan: 3 videos a month, one minute each — enough to see whether an avatar suits your audience.
3. **Test a training-style video** on Synthesia's free 10 minutes if you plan tutorials.
4. **Self-recorded videos** remain the cheapest option: film on your phone and caption with free tools.
5. **Measure** watch time and comments for two weeks before paying for anything.

The watermark is the main cost of staying free. For testing formats and hooks, it doesn't matter; for your best-performing format, upgrade only that one tool.

## How to choose in three questions

1. **Will you appear on camera?** No → avatar tool (HeyGen for social, Synthesia for training) plus a voice tool. Yes → an editor such as Descript.
2. **What length?** Under a minute, vertical → HeyGen. Several minutes, structured → Synthesia.
3. **How many languages?** Both HeyGen (175+ on paid) and Synthesia (140+ translations on Pro) cover French and English; compare the plan that includes translation.

## Common mistakes with AI video

- **Paying before testing the format.** Every tool here has a free tier or trial; use it to test hooks and formats first.
- **Ignoring credit consumption.** Both HeyGen and Synthesia now bill in credits; a longer or higher-resolution video costs more of your allowance.
- **Skipping captions.** Most short-form viewers watch without sound; add on-screen text.
- **No call to action.** End every video with one action: comment a keyword, visit one link.
