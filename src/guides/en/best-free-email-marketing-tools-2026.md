## Why "free" means different things

Free email plans are limited along four axes, and each vendor picks a different one:

1. **Subscribers/contacts** — how many people can be on your list.
2. **Sends** — how many emails you can send per day or month.
3. **Automation** — how many automated sequences or rules you can run.
4. **Features** — landing pages, forms, branding removal, integrations.

A plan that looks generous on one axis is often tight on another. The right free tool is the one whose limit you will hit last, given what you actually plan to do.

**Choose Brevo if:** you have a large contact base, send occasionally, or need transactional email.

## Compliance: the part free tools don't do for you

Whatever tool you choose, Canadian law (CASL) requires consent, identification of the sender and a working unsubscribe mechanism in every commercial email. U.S. law (CAN-SPAM) requires a valid physical postal address and an opt-out. All four tools provide unsubscribe links, but **you** are responsible for collecting consent properly and for putting your identification and address in the footer.

## Your first week on any free tool

The tool matters less than the first seven days. Whatever you pick:

1. **Write the promise** of your list in one sentence: who it is for and what they receive.
2. **Create one lead magnet** that solves a single problem in under ten minutes: a checklist, a template or a set of prompts.
3. **Build one signup form or page** with a single email field and clear consent wording.
4. **Write five welcome emails**: delivery, story, the tool you actually use, an honest comparison, a soft offer.
5. **Add your sender identification, postal address and unsubscribe link** to the footer template once, so every email inherits it.
6. **Point every piece of content to the signup page**: one link in bio, one niche English keyword under videos (LIST / EMAIL — see content/cta-keywords.md), one link in each article.
7. **Measure one number per week**, starting with the signup rate of your page.

Every free plan in this guide can run that entire week at $0.
