If you are launching with no budget, **start with Systeme.io**: it has a permanent free plan (2,000 contacts, 3 funnels, unlimited emails, no card), and its first paid tier is $17/month. **ClickFunnels** is the better-known brand with a larger training ecosystem and a richer feature list on paper, but it starts at **$97/month** (Launch plan, or $81/month billed annually) after a 14-day free trial. ## Scenarios: which should you pick?

**"I have no budget and no audience yet."** Systeme.io free. Build one opt-in page, one thank-you page, a 5-email sequence. Upgrade when you pass 2,000 contacts or need a fourth funnel.

**"I sell a course and want my students to bring affiliates."** Systeme.io, because the affiliate program is included at every tier. With ClickFunnels you would need Optimize ($297/month) for its built-in Affiliate Center.

**"I'm an established marketer with a team and several brands, and I value training and community."** ClickFunnels Scale or Optimize can make sense: more team members, workspaces, priority support and the training ecosystem.

**"I want to test ClickFunnels without risk."** Use the 14-day trial and the 30-day money-back guarantee. Build the same opt-in page in both tools and compare your own editing time.

## The same beginner funnel in both tools

To make the comparison concrete, here is the funnel from our free FUNNELS blueprint and what it costs to run in each tool.

**The funnel:** an opt-in page offering a free checklist, a thank-you page with a short video and a soft offer, one automation that delivers the checklist and tags the contact, and five welcome emails over seven days.

**In Systeme.io (free plan):** the opt-in and thank-you pages use one of your three funnels. The delivery automation uses your single automation rule. The five follow-up emails can run as your one campaign or as weekly broadcasts. Cost: $0, as long as you stay under 2,000 contacts.

**In ClickFunnels (Launch):** the same build fits easily inside Launch's limits (10,000 contacts, 50,000 emails per month, automations included). Cost: $97 per month, or $81 per month billed annually, after the 14-day trial.

**What changes as you grow:** with Systeme.io, the first pressure point is usually the single automation rule; you will want tag-based branches once you have two or three lead magnets, and that is when the $17 Startup plan becomes worth it. With ClickFunnels, the first pressure point is the monthly email cap and, if you want your own affiliates, the Affiliate Center that only appears on Optimize.

**Our take:** for this exact funnel, the extra $97 per month buys you nothing you need on day one. It may buy you training, onboarding calls and AI writing allowances, which some people value highly. Decide based on that, not on the funnel itself.
