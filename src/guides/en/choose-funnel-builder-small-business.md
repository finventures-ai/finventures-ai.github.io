## Who this guide is for

You sell a service, a course, a coaching package or a small digital product, and you keep hearing that you need "a funnel". You want to know what software you actually need, how much it should cost at your stage, and when an expensive platform starts to make sense.

This is not a ranking. It is a way to match a tool to the stage your business is in, so you neither overpay early nor outgrow a free plan at the worst moment. For a head-to-head look at two tools, see [Systeme.io vs ClickFunnels](/en/guides/systeme-io-vs-clickfunnels/) or [GoHighLevel vs Systeme.io](/en/vs/gohighlevel-vs-systeme-io/).

## What a first funnel really needs

Strip away the jargon and a first funnel is four pieces:

1. **A page that makes one offer** (a free guide, a consultation, a product).
2. **A form or checkout** that captures the email or the payment, with clear consent.
3. **A thank-you page** that delivers what was promised.
4. **A short email follow-up**, usually three to five messages.

Upsells, order bumps, A/B tests, pipelines and multi-step webinars are optimisations. They are valuable once traffic exists. Before that, they mostly add setup time.

## Stage 1: no budget, no audience yet

At this stage your biggest risk is spending money and weeks before you know whether anyone wants the offer. You want the four pieces above at $0.

**Systeme.io** is built for this stage. Its permanent free plan, with no card required, includes 2,000 contacts, 3 funnels, unlimited emails, 1 course, 1 blog and 1 custom domain. The limits that matter: one automation rule and one email campaign. That is enough for one funnel connected to one follow-up sequence.

ClickFunnels and GoHighLevel do not offer a permanent free plan; both offer a 14-day free trial. A trial is useful for evaluating a tool, but not for running a business that has not found its first customers yet.

## Stage 2: first customers, one or two offers

You have traffic and a few sales. You now want a second lead magnet with its own sequence, or a sales page plus a webinar. This is where free plans run out: Systeme.io's single automation rule is usually the first limit you hit.

Price ladder on Systeme.io (dollar amounts **VERIFIED** in our Nov 9, 2026 tool table): Startup **$17**/month, Webinar **$47**/month, Unlimited **$97**/month. For each paid tier's contact and funnel limits, check [systeme.io/pricing](https://systeme.io/pricing) directly. Unlimited email sends and 0% transaction fees on the free plan are in the same Nov 9 table.

ClickFunnels starts at Launch: $97/month, or $81/month billed annually. Launch lists 10,000 contacts and 50,000 emails a month. If you value its training ecosystem and built-in feature breadth, this can be the stage where it enters the discussion.

## Stage 3: a team, several brands, or client work

If you manage funnels for several brands or for clients, account structure matters more than page builders.

**GoHighLevel** is positioned around agencies and multi-client work. Its pricing FAQ lists Agency Starter at $97/month with 3 sub-accounts, Unlimited at $297/month with unlimited sub-accounts, and Pro/SaaS at $497/month; contacts and users are listed as unlimited, and usage-based fees for things like SMS, email and AI apply on top. We did not record annual billing figures or usage rates; check [GoHighLevel pricing](https://www.gohighlevel.com/pricing).

**ClickFunnels** scales workspaces with its tiers (Scale **$197**/month; Optimize **$297**/month; Dominate **$5,997**/year). Those monthly list prices are VERIFIED in our Nov 9 tool table.

Annual billing (effective monthly rates) for Scale and Optimize changes over time: confirm them on [clickfunnels.com/pricing](https://www.clickfunnels.com/pricing). For which tiers include an Affiliate Center (built-in affiliate program), check the same official page.

**Systeme.io** lists unlimited sub-accounts on every plan, which can suit a multi-brand solo operator.

## Questions to answer before you pay

1. **How many offers will I run in the next six months?** One offer rarely justifies $97 a month.
2. **Do I need my own affiliate program?** Systeme.io includes it on all plans; ClickFunnels lists it from Optimize.
3. **Am I serving clients?** If yes, sub-account structure (GoHighLevel, or Systeme.io's sub-accounts) matters more than templates.
4. **Can I export my contacts?** Whatever you choose, keep a regular CSV export of your list.
5. **Do I have my own sending domain?** Funnel emails need an authenticated domain to land reliably; Systeme.io, for one, no longer accepts a Gmail sender.

## Who this guide is not for

If you already pay for a website builder that handles your forms and checkout, you may only need an email tool; see our [email platform guide](/en/guides/choose-email-platform-automation-essentials/).
