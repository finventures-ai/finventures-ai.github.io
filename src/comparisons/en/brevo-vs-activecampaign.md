## Short answer

- **Choose Brevo** if you want to start at $0 with no card, send modest daily volumes (up to 300 emails a day on free), and manage contacts in one marketing platform.
- **Choose ActiveCampaign** if automation depth and a sales CRM are the reason you are buying software at all, and you can pay once the 14-day trial confirms the fit.
- **Both are business-oriented** rather than creator-oriented; the difference is mostly free versus trial, and breadth versus automation depth.

## The real difference: how you start, and how deep you go

**Brevo lets you start without paying.** A no-card free plan with a daily sending cap means you can build your contact base, set up basic automations and send real emails before spending anything. Its paid plans are described by email volume.

**ActiveCampaign asks you to evaluate, then pay.** There is no free plan. What you get in exchange is a product designed around automation: branching on behaviour, a CRM with deals and stages, and the reporting around them. It is a tool you choose because automation is central to how you sell.

If you cannot yet describe the automation you need, Brevo's free plan is a lower-risk way to find out. If you can describe it in detail, ActiveCampaign's trial is the fastest way to see whether it can be built.

## Free and trial use

**Brevo:** no card required; up to 300 emails a day after account approval. Our table records automations as available and CRM and landing pages as partial, without a per-plan split, so test the specific workflow you need.

**ActiveCampaign:** a 14-day trial with Pro-type features. Its help center describes a limited number of sends during the trial. The exact cap is not confirmed in our table: check the help center. Build your real workflow with test contacts during those two weeks.

## Paid plans

Both vendors show prices that change with your situation: Brevo by email volume (its marketing mentions tiers such as 5,000 emails a month), ActiveCampaign by contact count and plan (Starter, Plus, Pro, Enterprise). We did not confirm amounts for either, so we do not print them. Compare on [Brevo pricing](https://www.brevo.com/pricing) and [ActiveCampaign pricing](https://www.activecampaign.com/pricing) using the same contact count and monthly volume.

A practical tip: write down the same three numbers before opening either page (contacts, emails per month, users) so you compare like with like.

## Final verdict

The evidence: Brevo has a no-card free plan with a 300-a-day cap; ActiveCampaign has a 14-day trial and an automation-and-CRM core; neither price is confirmed in our sources. Our opinion: **start on Brevo if you are still discovering what you need; trial ActiveCampaign if you already know your automation is the business.**
