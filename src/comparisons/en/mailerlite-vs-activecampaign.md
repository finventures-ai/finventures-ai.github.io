## Short answer

- **Choose MailerLite** if you want an email tool that is simple to start, free up to 250 subscribers, and priced from $12/month when you grow, with sites and landing pages included.
- **Choose ActiveCampaign** if your follow-up depends on detailed behaviour-based automations and a CRM, and you are ready to pay once a 14-day trial proves it fits.
- **A useful test:** sketch your most complex automation on paper. If it has more than a couple of branches and involves sales stages, trial ActiveCampaign. If it is a welcome sequence and a newsletter, MailerLite is likely enough.

## Where they really differ

**MailerLite** treats email as a toolkit: campaigns, simple automations, forms, a site and landing pages, and on paid plans items such as digital products. Its pricing page says every paid plan has the full feature set and you pay for list size and sends. That predictability is a strength for small budgets.

**ActiveCampaign** treats email as part of customer automation. Its plans step up from Starter through Plus, Pro and Enterprise; landing pages appear from Plus. The CRM, deal stages and behaviour-driven automations are why businesses choose it. There is no permanent free plan.

In short: MailerLite makes the common things easy and cheap; ActiveCampaign makes the complex things possible, at a price you have to look up for your own contact count.

## Free and trial use

**MailerLite free:** 250 subscribers, 2,500 emails a month, three active automations of up to five steps, one site, one landing page, three forms and two users. On a free account, sending is blocked once active subscribers exceed 250, so plan your upgrade before then.

**ActiveCampaign trial:** 14 days with Pro-type features. Its help center describes a limited number of sends during the trial. The exact cap is not confirmed in our table: check the help center. Use it to build your real workflow end to end with test contacts.

## Paid plans

**MailerLite:** Comfort from **$12**/month, Power from **$25**/month (USD as displayed — Nov 9 table). For automation, form, site and user limits on each paid tier, check [mailerlite.com/pricing](https://www.mailerlite.com/pricing).

**ActiveCampaign:** the pricing page shows "starts at" amounts that depend on contacts and billing period. We have not confirmed a current figure, so we do not print one; see [ActiveCampaign pricing](https://www.activecampaign.com/pricing).

## Who should skip both

If you need funnels, a checkout and a course with email at $0, see our [funnel builder guide](/en/guides/choose-funnel-builder-small-business/). If you are a creator expecting thousands of subscribers on a free plan, see [Kit vs MailerLite](/en/guides/kit-vs-mailerlite/).

## Final verdict

The evidence: MailerLite has a free plan and published entry prices; ActiveCampaign has a trial and an automation-and-CRM core with prices that vary by contacts. Our opinion: **most small businesses should start on MailerLite and move only when an automation genuinely cannot be built there; sales-driven B2B teams should trial ActiveCampaign first.**
