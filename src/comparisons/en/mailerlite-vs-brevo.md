## Short answer

- **Choose MailerLite** if you are starting a small list and want the most documented tools on a free plan: a site, a landing page, forms and three automations. Plan to pay before you reach 250 subscribers; its first paid tier starts at $12/month.
- **Choose Brevo** if you send small batches spread over time to contacts you already have, and 300 emails a day is comfortable. No card is required.
- **Neither free plan is built for a weekly newsletter to a list of several thousand.** For that, compare [Kit vs MailerLite](/en/guides/kit-vs-mailerlite/).

## Which ceiling comes first?

Both plans are free forever, but they stop you in different places.

**MailerLite stops you at the list.** At 250 active subscribers, sending is blocked on a free account, for both campaigns and automations. Within that, 2,500 emails a month is ten sends to a full list.

**Brevo stops you at the day.** Up to 300 emails a day after account approval. Our table does not record a subscriber cap for Brevo's free plan, so we do not state one; check [Brevo pricing](https://www.brevo.com/pricing).

A simple way to decide: if your list will stay under 250 for the next six months, MailerLite's richer free toolkit probably wins. If you already have more contacts than that but email them in small groups, Brevo's model may suit you better.

## What the free plans include

### MailerLite

MailerLite is unusually explicit about its free plan: 250 subscribers, 2,500 emails a month, three active automations of up to five steps, one site and one landing page, three active forms, its editors, and two users. Its API access on free is limited and, per its page, excludes sending.

### Brevo

Brevo's free plan requires no card and allows up to 300 emails a day. Brevo positions itself as a broader marketing platform; our table records automations as available and landing pages and CRM as partial, without a per-plan breakdown.

## Paid plans

**MailerLite** prices by usage rather than features: per its pricing page, every paid plan has the full feature set and you pay more for a bigger list or more sends. Comfort starts at **$12**/month and Power at **$25**/month (USD as displayed — Nov 9 table). For any annual-billing discount, confirm the current percentage on [mailerlite.com/pricing](https://www.mailerlite.com/pricing).

**Brevo** describes Starter by monthly email volume (its marketing mentions tiers such as 5,000 emails a month). We did not record an amount; see [Brevo pricing](https://www.brevo.com/pricing).

## Product philosophy

**MailerLite** is a broad, simple toolkit for small businesses and creators: email plus sites, pages and, on paid plans, items such as digital products. Its marketing presents the editor as easy for beginners (vendor positioning; not tested by us).

**Brevo** is a general marketing platform where email is one channel among others, with contact management that leans towards CRM. That breadth suits businesses that want one place for customer communication; it is more than a newsletter needs.

## Final verdict

The evidence: MailerLite caps the list at 250 and documents a strong free toolkit; Brevo caps sending at 300 a day. Our opinion: **for a brand-new small list, start on MailerLite; for occasional sends to an existing customer base, Brevo's free plan can last longer.** Either way, check the paid tier for your real list size before you hit the ceiling.
