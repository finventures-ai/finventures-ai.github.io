## Short answer

- **Choose ClickFunnels** if you are a marketer or business owner running your own funnels and you value a funnel-centric product with a large training ecosystem. Launch is $97/month, or $81/month billed annually.
- **Choose GoHighLevel** if you run an agency or manage several client businesses: its plans are structured around sub-accounts, with Agency Starter at $97/month for 3 sub-accounts and Unlimited at $297/month.
- **The same entry price hides different bills.** ClickFunnels caps contacts and emails per plan; GoHighLevel lists unlimited contacts and users but adds usage-based fees for things such as SMS, email and AI.

## Built for two different operators

**ClickFunnels** is centred on the funnel: pages, checkout, upsells, courses and email inside one product, plus a large training and community ecosystem. Its pricing page lists unlimited funnels on all plans (flagged as limited-time), 3 courses on Launch and an AI allowance. Its Affiliate Center, for running your own affiliate program, appears only from Optimize.

**GoHighLevel** is centred on the agency: you resell or manage marketing software for multiple client businesses, each in its own sub-account. Its pricing FAQ lists unlimited contacts and users; the Pro/SaaS tier at $497/month is aimed at agencies that rebrand and resell the platform. Usage such as SMS, email and AI is billed on top.

If you are one business selling your own offers, GoHighLevel's sub-account structure is mostly unused capacity. If you serve ten clients, ClickFunnels' workspace limits become the constraint.

## Understanding the bill

**ClickFunnels' bill is mostly predictable.** You pick a tier; the tier sets contacts, monthly emails and workspaces. You outgrow it by contacts or sends, then move up.

**GoHighLevel's bill has two parts.** The subscription ($97, $297 or $497 per month) plus usage. We did not record usage rates, so estimate them from [GoHighLevel pricing](https://www.gohighlevel.com/pricing) for your expected SMS and email volume before comparing totals.

## Final verdict

The evidence: identical $97 entry points and 14-day trials; ClickFunnels caps by contacts and emails; GoHighLevel structures by sub-accounts and adds usage fees. Our opinion: **ClickFunnels for a single business that lives in its funnels; GoHighLevel for agencies serving several clients.** Use the trial to build the one thing you would do most often, and price the full bill, including usage, before deciding.
